# Auto-Dasher

**Your rules. Every offer. No staring at your phone.**

Auto-Dasher watches every DoorDash offer the second it lands and judges it by YOUR numbers: your minimum pay, your miles cap, and your dollars per mile. At $1.50 a mile or better it's GOOD. Between $1.00 and $1.50 it's a CLOSE call with a 30 second window to grab it. Under a dollar a mile it's a SKIP. In the automatic modes it accepts and declines for you, strictly by the rules you set, and every automatic action counts down on a banner first so one tap can cancel it.

## Install

1. Download the APK on your Android phone (Android 8 or newer, with the Dasher app).
2. Open the file and allow installs from your browser when Android asks.
3. Open Auto-Dasher and follow the one-page System Check: notifications, battery, and the accessibility watcher. The app explains the watcher in plain words before you turn it on, and nothing it sees ever leaves your phone.
