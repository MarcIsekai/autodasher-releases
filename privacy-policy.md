# Auto-Dasher Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Auto-Dasher is a companion app for DoorDash delivery drivers. This policy
explains, in plain language, what the app does with information. The
short version: nothing leaves your phone, because the app has no way to
send it anywhere.

## What the app accesses

With your permission, Auto-Dasher uses Android's Accessibility Service to
read the offer cards shown by the DoorDash Dasher app: the pay, distance,
estimated time, stops, store names, and shopping details on each offer.
It reads only the Dasher app. It does not read your other apps, messages,
contacts, photos, or passwords.

Before the accessibility feature can be turned on, the app shows a
disclosure explaining this, and it proceeds only if you tap "I
understand".

## What the app does with it

The offer details are compared, on your phone, against the rules you set
(minimum pay, miles cap, shopping limits, dollars-per-mile bands) to show
you a GOOD, SKIP, or CLOSE verdict. If you turn on an automatic mode, the
app taps Accept or Decline for you according to those same rules. Every
automatic action is announced on a countdown banner first, and tapping
the banner cancels it.

## What the app stores

Your settings are stored on your phone so the app remembers your rules.
If you turn on offer screenshots, a picture of each offer card is saved
to your own Pictures folder, on your phone. The app stores nothing else.

## What the app shares

Nothing. The Google Play version of Auto-Dasher does not include the
INTERNET permission, so it cannot transmit data anywhere. The app has no
accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party
tracking or SDKs. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal
information, because we never receive it.

## Children

Auto-Dasher is a work tool for adult delivery drivers. It is not directed
at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same
address, with a new effective date, before the change takes effect.
